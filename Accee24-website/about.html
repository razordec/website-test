About Access24

Architecting Enterprise Resilience and Growth

A Papua New Guinean-headquartered, family-owned system integrator helping organizations navigate modern IT with clarity and confidence. Part of the Bonhava Group, we combine entrepreneurial agility with global discipline.

Our Story

Built with Love. Engineered with Care.

Founded in 2019, we started with a simple belief: enterprises deserve a technology partner that combines deep expertise with genuine care for their success. Not just solutions — a partnership that protects today and powers what's next.

As both a VAR (Value-Added Reseller) and a VAD (Value-Added Distributor), we integrate top-notch technologies from the world's leading vendors to deliver solutions that are secure, scalable, and built to last.

Headquartered in Papua New Guinea, with offices in Port Moresby, Lae, and Alotau, we serve enterprises across Vaud, the greater Pacific region, and beyond — with teams with the Pacific Island style approach.
